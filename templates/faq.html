Frequently asked questions

Does the score guarantee ATS approval?

No. It is an educational estimate and different employers use different systems.

Which files are supported?

PDF, DOCX, and TXT. Scanned PDFs may not contain extractable text.

Are my files stored?

The current implementation processes files temporarily and attempts to delete them after processing. Review the code and hosting configuration before making privacy promises.
